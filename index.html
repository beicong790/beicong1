ABOUT US

专注高端铝镁锰金属屋面材料 Premium Al-Mg-Mn Metal Roofing Materials

我们是一家集研发、生产、销售与技术服务于一体的铝镁锰金属材料企业，致力于为全球客户提供稳定、耐候、安全的屋面系统材料。 We are an integrated manufacturer of Al-Mg-Mn metal materials, covering R&D, production, sales & technical service. We provide stable, weather-resistant & reliable roofing materials worldwide.

主营3003H24、3105H24铝镁锰基板，PVDF氟碳、HDP高耐候聚酯彩涂铝卷，以及直立锁边铝镁锰压型板。产品广泛应用于公共建筑、工业厂房、物流园区、机场、高铁站和体育馆等重点工程。 Main products: 3003H24 / 3105H24 Al-Mg-Mn substrate, PVDF & HDP prepainted aluminum coil, standing seam roof panels. Widely used in public buildings, factories, logistics parks, airports, high-speed rail stations and stadiums.
